There are two separate files here for the two different subjects of my work surrounding hydrogen infrastructure. 

The first "HydrogenNetworkOptimzation" details the strategy I developed to use NSGA-II optimization to align candidate sites and fleet depots to maximize fleet coverage, minimize total real estate cost, and minimize the total number of stations built. 

The second "SupplyChainTEA" details an techno-economic analysis of the total supply chain including production, geologic storage, distribution, and refueling for a 100,000 kilogram per day network. The production potential is evaluated by wind resources in Eastern Colorado.
